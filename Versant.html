  • ⚠️ Sit in an empty room — No other person should be present in the room.
  • ⚠️ No noise / silence — The room must be completely quiet with no background noise.
  • ⚠️ Blank wall behind you — Sit in front of a plain, blank wall (no posters, no windows, no people).

  • ⚠️ Keep your eyes on the computer — Do not look away from the screen at any time.
  • ⚠️ No cheating — Do not use any other device, notes, or take help from anyone.
  • ⚠️ Do not leave the screen — Stay on the exam tab until the exam is complete.
